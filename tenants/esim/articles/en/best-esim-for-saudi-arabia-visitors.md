## So, what is the best travel eSIM for Saudi Arabia?

There is no one best eSIM for every visitor. The right choice depends on how much data you need, whether you want a Saudi phone number, and how you plan to make calls. Start by checking that your phone supports eSIM, then compare plans on data size, validity, number options and registration steps.

If you want to browse options, see our [travel eSIM plans](/services/travel-esim-plans/).

## Check your phone first

Before comparing plans, confirm two things on your device: that it supports eSIM, and that it is not locked to a single carrier. Your phone settings or your manufacturer can tell you this. We cannot promise that any particular plan will work on any particular handset, so it is worth checking before you travel rather than on arrival.

## What should I look for in a Saudi Arabia eSIM plan?

Here is a practical checklist:

- **Data allowance and validity:** Match the amount of data to the length of your trip. As one example of a small starter option, Soovia lists a plan of 1 GB for 7 days at $3.90. Longer or heavier-use trips will need more.
- **A phone number or data only:** According to eSIMfly, a travel eSIM comes without a Saudi number, so Saudi bank and government one-time codes still go to your home line. Keep your home SIM active for those. Some providers do offer plans that include a number; Cybernews says Airalo offers some Saudi plans with a phone number for calls and SMS.
- **Registration steps:** Find out what identity checks apply before you land.
- **Your calling habits:** See the WhatsApp section below.
- **Network performance data:** Useful as a guide, but not a promise for your trip.

## Do I need to register my eSIM in Saudi Arabia?

Possibly, so it is wise to plan for it. Simology warns that SIMs or eSIMs left unregistered may be deactivated within 24 to 72 hours of use. That warning is not confirmed by CST, so treat it as a reason to check your provider's steps, not as a firm rule.

On the official side, CST announced that international pilgrims could activate eSIM cards through service providers' apps. Saudi Gazette says this removes the need to visit service branches on arrival.

For a local carrier visitor eSIM, eSIMfly says the stc option needs the mystc app plus Absher verification, with a passport and border number. Have those details to hand if you go this route.

## Is a local carrier eSIM better than a travel eSIM?

Not necessarily; they suit different people. A local carrier option can be a good fit if you are comfortable with an app and identity verification. Traveltomtom recommends an STC prepaid SIM card in 2026. A travel eSIM from a store can be simpler to install before departure, though you should read the plan details for number and registration information.

## What does network performance data say?

Public network reports can help you compare carriers, but results differ by report, metric and location, and they do not predict your own experience.

| Report | Finding |
|---|---|
| Opensignal, Feb 2026 | 5G download speed: stc 260.2 Mbps, Zain 242.7 Mbps, Mobily 224.4 Mbps |
| Opensignal, Oct 2025 | Mobily led 5G Video Experience at 74.5, against stc 71.6 and Zain 69.5 |

The lesson is that different carriers lead on different measures. Use this as one input, not a deciding factor, and do not expect a specific speed.

## Will WhatsApp calls work on an eSIM?

Probably not for voice and video. DriftAsia says WhatsApp voice and video calls are blocked in Saudi Arabia, while text and voice notes work. YonoSIM adds that a travel eSIM does not fix the WhatsApp-call block. If calls matter to you, consider a plan that includes a number for calls and SMS, or plan to message instead.

## A simple way to decide

1. Confirm your phone is eSIM-compatible and unlocked.
2. Decide whether you need a Saudi number or only data.
3. Estimate your data needs for the length of your trip.
4. Check the registration and identity steps for your chosen plan.
5. Keep your home SIM active for one-time codes.
6. Install the eSIM before you travel if the provider allows it.
